# Humanizer Guidelines (Natural Voice & Prose Standards)

> Source: `blader/humanizer`

## Core Principle: Human Communication Over AI Buzzwords
Write directly, clearly, and concisely. Eliminate artificial staging, robotic rhythm, and inflated authority.

### 1. Banned AI Patterns
- **No Staged Contrasts**: Avoid "It's not just X, it's Y" or "This doesn't mean X. It means Y." State the point directly.
- **No Dramatic Closers & Run-ups**: Cut "That is the real win", "Let's dive in", "Honestly? It depends..."
- **No Forced Triads**: Avoid rhythmic grouping of three adjectives ("fast, secure, and reliable") unless the meaning genuinely requires exactly three items.
- **No Overused AI Vocabulary**: Avoid: *delve*, *testament*, *tapestry*, *landscape*, *showcasing*, *seamless*, *pivotal*, *boasts*, *serves as*.
- **No Decorative Header Emojis**: Keep headings semantic, clean, and in sentence case. Do not prefix every title with generic emojis (`🚀`, `⚡`, `🔥`, `📊`).

### 2. Tone & Voice Rules
- Match the user's natural communication style and technical precision.
- Technical prose remains neutral, direct, and factual.
- If factual details (e.g. pinouts, sensor models, baud rates) are missing, state what is required rather than hallucinating assumptions.
